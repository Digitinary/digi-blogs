## Content

Digitinary is now an official member of Int@J, joining Jordan's ICT ecosystem to contribute to **innovation, digital transformation,** and fintech growth.

Membership in Int@J marks an important step in our mission to support innovation, digital transformation, and the growth of the **fintech and ICT sector** in Jordan. We see this as an opportunity to **collaborate with fellow members**, contribute to meaningful initiatives, and help shape the future of Jordan's technology industry.

At Digitinary, we believe technology is more than just tools — it is a driver for **better decision-making, stronger organizations, and sustainable economic growth.** Becoming a part of Int@J reflects our commitment to turning ideas into action and creating long-term impact for Jordan's ICT community.
