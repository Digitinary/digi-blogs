## Content

Digitinary participated in **JO the FinTech Festival 2025**, co-exhibiting with the Central Bank of Jordan.

This collaboration highlighted our continued commitment to advancing **financial innovation** and **digital transformation** across the region.

**FivHive, Jordan's leading Open Banking Aggregator**, was showcased as a key solution simplifying Open Banking connectivity and driving fintech innovation.

Digitinary continues to drive progress in **Open Banking** and **digital finance**, empowering institutions to build smarter, more connected financial services.

To explore Digitinary's Open Banking solution, visit [finhive.tech](https://finhive.tech/)
