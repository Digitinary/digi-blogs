# Digitinary Achieves ISO 27001 Certification, Reinforcing Security First Culture

**Date:** March 11, 2025
**Slug:** digitinary-iso-27001-certification
**Image:** /images/news/ISOCertificate.png

## Description

Digitinary has officially obtained ISO 27001 certification, reinforcing its security-first culture and commitment to data protection, information security, compliance, and the highest standards in financial technology.

## Content

Believing in building a **security-first culture**, Digitinary is proud to announce that we have officially obtained the **ISO 27001 certification!**

**Security and compliance** are at the core of everything we do. This certification is a testament to our commitment to **protecting data**, ensuring **information security**, and maintaining the highest industry standards in **financial technology**.
