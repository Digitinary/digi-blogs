# Digitinary Becomes the First Jordanian Company Accepted into JoRegBox

**Date:** January 25, 2025
**Slug:** digitinary-first-jordanian-joregbox
**Image:** /images/news/AggregatorCertificate.png

## Description

Digitinary becomes the first Jordanian company accepted into JoRegBox by the Central Bank of Jordan as an Open Banking and Open Finance Aggregator.

## Content

Digitinary becomes the **first Jordanian company** accepted into JoRegBox by the Central Bank of Jordan as an Open Banking and Open Finance Aggregator, supporting fintech innovation and financial inclusion.

This milestone represents a major step forward in our mission to empower the **fintech sector** and **small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs)** in Jordan, while accelerating innovation in **Open Banking** and **Open Finance**.

Acceptance into JoRegBox highlights Digitinary's commitment to **digital transformation**, **financial inclusion**, and the development of secure, innovative financial solutions that align with regulatory standards.
